Stress Analysis
Axial Stress
σ = F/A
F=force (N), A=area (m²)
Bending Stress
σ = M·c/I
M=moment, c=distance from NA, I=2nd moment
Transverse Shear
τ = VQ/(It)
V=shear, Q=first moment, t=width at point
Torsion Shear
τ = Tr/J
J=π d⁴/32 (solid); angle φ=TL/GJ
Principal Stresses
σ₁,₂ = σ_avg ± √(((σx-σy)/2)²+τ²)
σ_avg=(σx+σy)/2; R=√(...)
von Mises
σ_vm = √(σ₁²-σ₁σ₂+σ₂²)
Yield when σ_vm = Sy; n=Sy/σ_vm
Tresca
τ_max = (σ₁-σ₂)/2 ≤ Sy/2
More conservative than von Mises
Stress Concentration
σ_max = Kt·σ_nom
Kt from Peterson's charts; Kf=1+q(Kt-1)
